## Notes for future cities
When researching a new city, follow this same structure:
1. Identify the city's primary gay district (search "[city] gay district 2026")
2. Search hotel guide sites (TravelGay, NomadicBoys, thegaypassport, misterb&b) for hotels explicitly recommended for LGBTQ+ travelers near that district
3. Cross-check with places_search centered on the district for star-rating-appropriate options if guide sites don't cover all three tiers
4. For luxury (5★) tiers in cities where no gay-specific guide recommends one, search "[city] only 5-star hotel" or "Forbes 5-star [city]" — gay travel guides often skip true luxury tiers, so this needs a separate search
5. Always note proximity (walk time preferred, transit time as fallback) to the gay district specifically, not just "central" or "downtown"
